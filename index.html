NBA Prop Quant

XGBoost, scikit-learn
DuckDB, pydantic
BALLDONTLIE data
Private repository,
walkthrough on request

Pregame fair prices for ten player-prop markets: points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, made threes and four combinations.

StepWhat it does
Mean modelsMinutes and per-stat means fit walk-forward on 273,082 out-of-fold rows, seasons 2001 to 2025.
DistributionsMean-preserving zero-inflated negative binomial for every single stat.
CombinationsRebounds + assists use a dependence setting of 0.85, chosen by grouped cross-validation; the other combinations use 0.
CalibrationBy prop type, fit only on earlier dates. Steals and blocks are left raw.
FreezeLocked on 18 August 2026, before the season, so 2026-27 is an untouched test. No betting threshold was tuned on past ROI.

It was developed against 145,083 priced 2025 sportsbook props, and the 2026-27 season is graded prospectively against the devigged market.

02 · pricing model

Soccer scorelines

penaltyblog, BALLDONTLIE
Docker, GitHub Actions
EPL source
World Cup source

Both engines are built around one object: the joint grid P(home goals = x, away goals = y). Every market (1X2, totals, both teams to score, correct score, handicaps) is read from that grid, before the match and live.

Premier League

Five goal models fit with time-decay weights on walk-forward train, validation and test windows: Weibull copula, Dixon-Coles, bivariate Poisson, negative binomial and zero-inflated Poisson. Pi ratings supply the prior. The ensemble weights come from validation log-loss (currently 0.30, 0.22, 0.18, 0.18 and 0.12 in that order), and a temperature parameter calibrates the grid. A live updater turns the pregame grid into an in-play grid from BALLDONTLIE match events.

World Cup 2026

The same grid for every match of the tournament, built on a composite team prior (market odds, FIFA ranking, qualifying form, confederation strength) and reconciled to the no-vig market by minimum KL divergence. With only four to eight matches per team, the reconciliation does the heavy lifting: it keeps the model's shape while matching the market's information, and it updates live during the match.

03 · pricing model

NFL player props

BALLDONTLIE NFL API
127 tests
CI-enforced contracts

Every prop for a game comes from one simulation of that game, so the props cannot contradict each other.

Most prop models are a stack of separate regressions, which is how a quarterback ends up projected for 260 passing yards while his receivers add up to 291. Here the game is simulated play by play: environment, availability, dropbacks and rushes, targets, completions, scoring, overtime if tied. A quarterback's passing yards are the sum of the receiving gains on his completions, and that identity is checked on every simulated game; a violation stops the run.

Built for the 2026-27 season against the BALLDONTLIE NFL API.

05 · market tools

Scanning, quoting, paper trading

Odds scanner

Pre-game scanner on The Odds API for positive-EV prices, arbitrage and middles. Four devig methods, prices pooled across books in logit space, fractional Kelly on the pooled fair price, CLV tracked against stored fair prices, and a staleness gate. 42 tests. For a book it is the same scan in reverse: where your own prices lag.

Kalshi market making

Two-sided quoting with signed API access (RSA-PSS), live WebSocket order books, a model of Kalshi's quadratic fee, and a check for adverse selection: fills that keep coming from traders who know more than the quote. That is the question a risk desk asks about an account.

NBA in-play trader

A possession-level Markov model of NBA games feeding an automated player-prop trader on Kalshi. It runs in paper mode and is graded on every market before any real stake goes in.

06 · live execution

NCAAB in-play engine

Python, Numba, asyncio
ESPN, Odds API, KenPom
Novig and Kalshi orders
136 automated tests
Code walkthrough on request

My live trading system. It prices every Division I game in progress every 10 seconds and can send orders to Novig or Kalshi. Team strength comes from KenPom ratings; what I built is the possession simulation, the live updating, and above all the risk controls around execution.

Live since 14 November 2026.

Each possession runs through a simulation of the NCAA rules (bonus at seven and ten team fouls, second-half fouls carried into overtime), and the box score moves the team rates during the game with credibility weights, n/(n + k), the same form an actuary uses to blend new claims experience with a manual rate. 25,000 simulations per game give the full distribution of the final score.

Risk controls

Most of the code that touches money decides when not to bet.

ControlRule
Stake0.5% of bankroll per wager until the gate passes; 1.5% once it passes on 300 signals; 3.0% if it still passes at 600. Set at startup, never mid-slate, and it falls back to 0.5% if a later slate fails.
Go-live gate300 settled signals, positive mean CLV, score distributions a PIT test does not reject, model Brier no worse than the devigged sharp market.
Exposure14% of bankroll per game, 60% open. A game's exposure stays locked for 15 minutes after it drops off the ESPN feed, which loses games for minutes at a time.
PositionsOne per game, market and side at any line. The other side is blocked. A second bet only on a strong signal, at least 180 s later, never at a worse line.
Data guardsThe whole game stops when the model and the sharp line differ by more than 3 points on the spread or 8 on the total. Late blowouts, unclear possession, lagging play-by-play and stale quotes stop it too.
Sanity ceilingAn edge above 20% or EV above 35% is a data error, never a bet.
Venue checksA price clearly worse than the sharp reference is skipped and the other venue tried. Every order is written to the ledger before it is sent; an unknown order state is never re-sent.
Dry runRuns the same rules as real orders and splits only at the venue call, so a dry run shows what a real night would do.

A book's open bets are a liability, and pricing them is the problem I worked on for ten years in insurance.

Through FBAC LLC I value retiree-health liabilities for Louisiana public employers, including several parish sheriffs' offices. The automation is more than 4,000 lines of Python across eight modules, handles funded and unfunded plans, and ties out to the cent against the prior report before anything reaches a client. 08 · approach

How I run risk

  • Price from a distribution, never from a point estimate. Correlated legs, pushes and team totals all come from the same joint distribution.
  • Grade every decision against the market, not the result. CLV and Brier against the devigged sharp price tell you in weeks what P&L takes a season to say.
  • Size by credibility. A new model, a new market or a new account starts small and earns room as its sample grows.
  • Treat big edges as data problems first. When a model and a sharp market disagree by a lot, the model is usually the one that is wrong.
  • Write the rule down and test it. A limit that lives in someone's head fails at 7:05 on a Saturday with 40 games live.
