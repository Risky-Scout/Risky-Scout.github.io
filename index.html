NCAAB live trading engine and risk controls

Prices every live Division I game every 10 seconds (full game, halves, team totals) from a possession-level simulation, with team ratings from KenPom and in-game credibility updating from the box score. Orders route to Novig and Kalshi through the controls below.

Stake per bet steps up only as the model earns it

The gate: positive mean CLV, a model Brier score no worse than the devigged sharp market, and score distributions a PIT test does not reject. It is re-checked every morning after settlement, and the stake drops back to 0.5% if a later slate fails.
